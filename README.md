# Target Dummy — Measure Your Exact Combat Damage

Target Dummy 9minecraft Mod gives you a placeable training dummy to test your weapons and measure your combat skills. This tool lets you hit the dummy to see exactly how much damage each attack does, helping you compare different swords, bows, and potions.

Floating Damage Numbers: Pops up colorful numbers above the dummy every time you hit it so you can read your exact damage. Live DPS Tracker: Shows a damage-per-second readout to help you compare fast attacks against slow, heavy hits.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **StormwyrmStudios**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
